logger - put a message in the system log

USAGE
    logger [-t TAG] [-p [FACILITY.]PRIO] [-s] [-f FILE] [MESSAGE...]

    Sends MESSAGE (or each line of stdin, or of FILE) to logd. The tag
    is TAG, or your user name; the priority notice unless -p says
    (0-7, emerg ... debug; the facility is accepted and ignored). -s
    prints it on stderr too. logd records your pid and user.

EXAMPLES
    logger backup done
    logger -t backup -p err "the disk is full"
    logs -t backup
