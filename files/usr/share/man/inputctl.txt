inputctl - the input service's settings

USAGE
    inputctl [status]                   the settings
    inputctl set-pointer-speed PERCENT  scale mouse movement (25-400; 100
                                        as the mouse reports it)

    Kept in /etc/input.conf; the input service takes a change at once.
    Root's (sudo inputctl ...). Settings > Mouse uses it.
