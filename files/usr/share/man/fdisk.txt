fdisk - list partition tables

USAGE
    fdisk -l [DISK...]

    Lists each disk (all of them without DISKs): its size and model, its
    partition table (GPT or MBR, read from the disk itself) and the
    partitions with their first and last sectors and type. To change a
    table, use gpt.

SEE ALSO
    gpt, lsblk, blkid
