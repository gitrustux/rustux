users - users, groups and permissions on Rustica (step 17)

    Every process acts as a user, files have owners and permission bits,
    and the filesystems enforce them. root (uid 0) may do anything.

    The accounts are in /etc/passwd, /etc/group and /etc/shadow (only
    root may read it; SHA-512 password hashes). People sign in at the
    login prompt; the live system logs root in by itself (/etc/autologin).

COMMANDS
    id, whoami, groups      who you are
    login                   sign in (init runs it on the console)
    su [-] [USER]           another user's shell (its password)
    sudo CMD                CMD as root (your password; /etc/sudoers)
    passwd [USER]           change a password
    useradd, userdel, usermod, groupadd, groupdel, chpasswd
    chmod, chown, chgrp     modes and owners (ls -l shows them)

EXAMPLES
    useradd -m -G sudo alice     a user who may use sudo
    passwd alice                 her password
    su - alice                   become her
    sudo poweroff                as her: root's command

FILES
    /etc/passwd /etc/group /etc/shadow /etc/sudoers /etc/autologin
    /var/log/auth.log (su and sudo)
