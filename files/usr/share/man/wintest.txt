wintest - a test window

USAGE
    wintest NAME [-c RRGGBB] [-s WxH] [-p X,Y] [-a] [-n] [-k] [-t TEXT]

    Opens a window filled with a color, its name and size, and prints
    every event it gets. -s size, -p position, -a animate (paced by the
    compositor's frames), -n ignore close requests, -k key c copies TEXT
    (-t) to the clipboard and key v pastes. Typed text shows in the window.

SEE ALSO
    compositor, rustica-window
