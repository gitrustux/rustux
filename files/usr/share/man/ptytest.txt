ptytest - test pseudo-terminals

USAGE
    ptytest

    Runs a program of its own, then the shell, on pseudo-terminals
    (posix_openpt) and checks the size (TIOCSWINSZ, TIOCGWINSZ), echo,
    Backspace, cooked and raw mode, Ctrl+C and the end (EIO). Prints
    "ptytest: ok" when all is well; runtests terminal runs it.

SEE ALSO
    terminal, runtests
