passwd - change a password

USAGE
    passwd [USER]

    Changes your password: the current one first, then the new one
    twice. root may change anyone's (no current password asked). The
    password is kept as a SHA-512 hash in /etc/shadow. passwd asks authd,
    which prompts on your terminal.

    A new account's password is locked until it is set; usermod -L and
    -U lock and unlock one; chpasswd sets many (as root, USER:PASSWORD
    lines on standard input).
