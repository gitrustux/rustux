soundctl - the volume, and sound from the console

USAGE
    soundctl [status]
    soundctl volume [N | +N | -N]
    soundctl mute | unmute | toggle
    soundctl test
    soundctl tone HZ SECONDS [RATE [CHANNELS]]
    soundctl play FILE.wav        (also: play FILE.wav)

    Talks to the sound service, soundd (svc status sound), which mixes
    every program's sound into the sound card's. Anyone may use it.

    status      the volume (0-100), whether it is muted, the sound devices
                (the one in use marked) and their outputs: speaker,
                headphones and line, each on, off (headphones are in: the
                speakers are turned off) or unplugged
    volume N    set the volume (0-100); +N and -N change it. The panel's
                volume, Settings > Sound and the volume keys set the same
    mute, unmute, toggle
    test        a short chord: left, right, then both
    tone        a sine tone: HZ for SECONDS, made at RATE (48000) in
                CHANNELS (2), for checking the conversion of rates
    play        a WAV file: 16-bit PCM, 1 or 2 channels, any rate from
                8000 to 192000 Hz

    The volume and muting are kept in /var/lib/sound/state.

DEVICES
    Intel High Definition Audio, the sound of nearly every PC (the hda
    driver: speakers, headphones and line out; HDMI and DisplayPort sound
    not yet). dmesg | grep hda shows what was found.
