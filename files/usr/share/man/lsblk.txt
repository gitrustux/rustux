lsblk - list block devices

USAGE
    lsblk [-b] [-n] [-l] [-d] [-f] [-o COLUMNS] [-J] [NAME...]

    Lists the disks and their partitions as a tree: NAME, RM (removable),
    SIZE, RO (read-only), TYPE (disk or part), MOUNTPOINTS (none yet).
    Disks appear when their driver starts: vda (virtio), nvme0n1 (NVMe),
    ram0 ("ramdisk 16M &").

    -b  sizes in bytes          -n  no header line
    -l  a list, not a tree      -d  disks only
    -f  filesystems: FSTYPE, LABEL, UUID
    -o  columns, e.g. -o NAME,SIZE,PARTLABEL (+COL adds to the default;
        lsblk --help lists them)
    -J  JSON (also --json), partitions as "children"

SEE ALSO
