lsblk - list block devices

USAGE
    lsblk [-b] [-n] [-J|--json]

    Lists the block devices published to the device manager: NAME, RM
    (removable), SIZE, RO (read-only), TYPE. Disks appear when their driver
    starts; "ramdisk 16M &" makes one in memory (ram0).

    -b  sizes in bytes
    -n  no header line
    -J  JSON (also --json)

    Nothing is mounted from block devices yet (filesystems come in steps
    14 to 16), so MOUNTPOINTS is empty.

SEE ALSO
    lspci, ramdisk --help
