localectl - the keyboard layout

USAGE
    localectl [status]
    localectl list-keymaps
    localectl set-keymap NAME

    set-keymap writes /etc/keymap and the console uses the layout at
    once (as loadkeys does). Layouts: us and those in
    /usr/share/keymaps. Changes are root's: sudo localectl set-keymap de.
    The locale is always C.UTF-8.
