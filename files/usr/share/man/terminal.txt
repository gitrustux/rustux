terminal - the shell in a window

USAGE
    terminal                 a window with the shell (/bin/shell)
    terminal -e PROG ARGS    PROG instead

    The program runs on a pseudo-terminal: the console's escape sequences
    (colours, the cursor, the alternate screen of top and editors) and a
    scrollback of 5000 lines. A tab closes when its program ends, the
    window with the last tab.

KEYS
    Ctrl+Shift+T            a new tab (also the + button)
    Ctrl+Shift+W            close the tab (also its x)
    Ctrl+PgUp, Ctrl+PgDn    the tab before, after
    Ctrl+Shift+C            copy what the mouse selected
    Ctrl+Shift+V            paste (also the middle button)
    Ctrl+= Ctrl+- Ctrl+0    bigger, smaller, normal text
    Shift+PgUp, Shift+PgDn  scroll back (also the wheel)
