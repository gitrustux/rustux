settings - the desktop's settings

USAGE
    settings [SECTION]   display, keyboard, mouse, time, users, disks, about

    Display: the scale of windows (~/.config/rustica.conf, scale=).
    Keyboard: the layout (localectl). Mouse: the pointer speed (inputctl).
    Date & Time: the clock and the time zone (timedatectl). Users: add,
    remove, make administrator (useradd, userdel, usermod, chpasswd).
    Disks: what lsblk shows. About: the system and the hardware.

    What is the system's is root's: another user types their password
    and the change is made with sudo -S (they must be in group sudo).
