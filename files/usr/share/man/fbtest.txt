fbtest - draw through the display service

USAGE
    fbtest [-n FRAMES] [--pattern SECONDS] [--check]

    Takes the screen from the text console (the display service gives it
    to root only) and draws colour bars with rectangles moving over them,
    presenting only what changed, then reports the frame rate and the time
    a frame takes to draw and to present. Esc or q ends it early.
    Ctrl+Alt+F1 shows the text console meanwhile, Ctrl+Alt+F7 the
    graphics again. When fbtest ends the console comes back.

    -n FRAMES          how many frames (300)
    --pattern SECONDS  a still test pattern for SECONDS (to compare
                       screenshots), then end
    --check            draw the pattern, take a snapshot through the
                       service and compare it with what was drawn
