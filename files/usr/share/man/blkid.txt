blkid - what block devices hold

USAGE
    blkid [-o full|value|export] [-s TAG]... [NAME...]

    Prints the tags of each block device (all of them without NAMEs):
      PTTYPE, PTUUID        a disk's partition table (gpt or dos)
      TYPE, LABEL, UUID     a filesystem: vfat, exfat, ntfs, BitLocker,
                            ext2, ext3, ext4, swap, iso9660
      PARTLABEL, PARTUUID   a GPT partition's name and GUID
    Names are devmgr's (vda1), not /dev paths.
