evtest - print input events

USAGE
    evtest [-t SECONDS]

    Takes the focus from the input service, so what is typed goes to
    evtest alone, and prints every event: keys (HID usage, down, up or
    repeat, the modifiers, and the text the keyboard layout makes of a
    press) and the pointer (relative motion or an absolute position,
    buttons, wheel). Esc ends it (or SECONDS passing); the focus goes back
    to where it was. Root only.
