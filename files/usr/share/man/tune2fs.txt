tune2fs - show an ext2/3/4 filesystem's superblock, or relabel it

USAGE
    tune2fs -l DEVICE
    tune2fs -L LABEL DEVICE

    -l prints what the superblock says, as Linux's tune2fs -l: the
    label, UUID, features, state, counts of blocks and inodes (and free
    ones), sizes, times, the journal inode, the directory hash and the
    checksum. -L gives the filesystem a new label (16 characters at
    most); not while it is mounted.

EXAMPLES
    tune2fs -l sda2 | grep features
    tune2fs -L backup sda2
