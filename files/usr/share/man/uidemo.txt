uidemo - the toolkit's demo

USAGE
    uidemo [--scale N] [X,Y]

    A window built with rustica-ui, Rustica's toolkit: a menu bar (F10),
    tabs with a form, a list of 1000 items and a tree, a text editor field
    and images, and dialogs. Tab moves the focus, Enter and Space press
    buttons, arrows move in lists and trees, Ctrl+C and Ctrl+V copy and
    paste. --scale 2 doubles everything. Needs the compositor.
