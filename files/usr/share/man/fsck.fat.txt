fsck.fat - check a FAT filesystem (also fsck.vfat)

USAGE
    fsck.fat [-a] [-v] DEVICE

    Checks a FAT filesystem that is not mounted: the boot sector, that the
    FAT copies agree, every directory and cluster chain (clusters in range,
    no loops or shared clusters, sizes matching chains, "." and ".."),
    long-name entries, clusters that no file owns, and FSInfo's free count.
    It repairs nothing, except that -a corrects a wrong FSInfo free count.
    -v lists every file. Exit status: 0 no errors, 1 corrected, 4 errors.

EXAMPLES
    umount /boot/efi; fsck.fat sda1; mount /boot/efi

SEE ALSO
    mkfs.fat, mount
