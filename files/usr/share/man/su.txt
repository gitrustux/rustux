su - become another user

USAGE
    su [-] [-l] [USER] [-c COMMAND]

    Starts USER's shell (root's if no USER), or runs COMMAND with it,
    after USER's password (root needs none). With - or -l the shell gets
    USER's login environment and starts in its home; without, your
    environment is kept (HOME, SHELL, USER, LOGNAME are USER's) and the
    working directory too. Exit the shell to come back.

    su asks authd, the system's authentication service, which checks
    the password on your terminal. Uses are logged in /var/log/auth.log.

EXAMPLES
    su                    root's shell (root's password)
    su - alice            alice's login shell
    su alice -c 'id'      one command as alice
