viewer - the Image Viewer

USAGE
    viewer FILE

    A PNG or JPEG picture, made smaller to fit the window. Left and Right
    show the other pictures of its folder it can see; Esc closes.

    An app (org.rustica.imageviewer, which comes with the system): it runs
    in its sandbox. Files opens pictures with it (appctl open), giving it
    that picture alone.
