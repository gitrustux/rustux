compositor - windows on the screen

USAGE
    compositor [-v]

    Takes the screen and, while graphics are shown, the keyboard and
    mouse, and lets programs open windows (wintest, rustica-window). Click
    to focus and raise; drag the title bar to move, the right or bottom
    edge to resize; the title bar's buttons minimise, maximise and close;
    Alt+Tab switches windows, Alt+F4 closes one, F11 makes it full screen.
    Ctrl+Alt+F1 shows the text console, Ctrl+Alt+F7 the windows again.
    -v prints what happens. Root only.

SEE ALSO
