tar - create, extract and list tar archives

USAGE
    tar -cf ARCHIVE FILE...     create (add -z for gzip: .tar.gz)
    tar -xf ARCHIVE [FILE...]   extract (-C DIR: into DIR)
    tar -tf ARCHIVE             list (-v: with sizes; --json)

    The dash may be left out: tar czf backup.tgz /root
    -O extracts to standard output; --exclude=GLOB leaves files out.
    Archives are read with the tar format (ustar/GNU) and gzip.

SEE ALSO
    gzip --help
