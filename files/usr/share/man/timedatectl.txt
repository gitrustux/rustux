timedatectl - the clock and the time zone

USAGE
    timedatectl [status]
    timedatectl list-timezones
    timedatectl set-timezone ZONE
    timedatectl set-time "YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS"     (or "HH:MM:SS"; local time)
    timedatectl set-local-rtc 0|1

    status shows the local and universal time and the zone. The zone is
    in /etc/timezone (names from list-timezones). set-local-rtc 1 says
    the hardware clock keeps local time, as Windows does (/etc/adjtime;
    read at boot). Changes are root's: sudo timedatectl ...
